Memory hooks

  • Same meaning as normal verbs. Deponent just means a different set of endings.
  • Present and future are identical apart from the σ, just like λυω.
  • Ends in -ι? Present or future (-μαι, -ται, -νται). No ι? A past tense (-μην, -το, -ντο).
  • Only the 2nd singular really changes: -η (present, future), -ου (imperfect), -ω (aorist). Say “η, ου, ω”.
  • Vowel sounds: ο and ε in the present, future and imperfect; α in the aorist.
  • The σθ is the fingerprint: -εσθε, -ασθε (you pl.), -εσθαι, -ασθαι (infinitives), and -εσθε in the present imperative 2 pl.
  • Participles have -μεν-: ῥυομενος. Spot -μεν- and think deponent or middle.
  • Watch out: the 2nd plural present imperative equals the present indicative (ῥυεσθε), and ἀρχομαι and ἐρχομαι look identical in the imperfect (ἠρχομην).
