Thank You for Standing With Me

A reflection on this summer's CCBR internship, what God has been teaching me, and the people who made the work possible.

Dear friends and family,

I wanted to take a moment to update you on everything God has done this summer and to thank you again for making it possible.

We have had many conversations this summer on street corners and doorsteps. We have seen people move toward the pro-life position simply by being shown the truth they had never considered before.

In one conversation, I explained that a new human life begins at fertilization. If it is wrong to kill a child after birth, it should also be wrong to kill that same child before birth. The child's age, size and location may change, but what the child is does not.

That simple argument made the problem of abortion much clearer. Difficult circumstances deserve real compassion and support, but they do not change the humanity of the unborn child or remove that child's right to life.

By God's grace, we have heard of at least three babies who were saved this summer. Three lives are here today because someone saw our signs and was willing to reconsider what they believed about abortion. Most of the time, we do not know what happens after someone walks away from a conversation or drives past one of our signs. We simply show up, speak the truth and trust God with the results.

Another encouraging moment was visiting a local Pregnancy Care Centre. We learned more about how they support women and families through counselling, practical resources and compassionate care. It reminded me that being pro-life is also about walking alongside mothers and fathers and helping them receive the support they need to choose life.

A Challenging Summer

It has also been a very challenging summer. We have been harassed and yelled at. There have been moments when showing up took more courage than I expected. But even in those moments, I have been reminded that this work was never meant to be comfortable, but it would be worth it.

I have also learned so much. I have become a better communicator and pro-life ambassador, gained a better understanding of how our culture arrived here and learned from the social reform movements that came before us.

The People

One of the greatest gifts of this internship has been the people. I have met courageous young people, from across Ontario, Quebec and Calgary, united around a common cause. We have laughed together, talked about real things and worked hard together through the hot summer days.

I have also had the chance to share this journey publicly through social media. I hope it has encouraged others in my own circle to speak up, have difficult conversations and stand against injustice, even when it costs something.

God's Provision

This summer has stretched me in other ways too. Balancing this internship with training, school, ministry and staying present with friends and family has required me to steward my time carefully. It has not always been easy, but I have felt God's provision every step of the way.

“Let us not grow weary of doing good, for in due season we will reap, if we do not give up.” Galatians 6:9

I am deeply grateful for your prayers and support this summer. None of this would have been possible without you standing behind me. Please continue to keep me and this movement in your prayers as the work continues beyond this summer.

With gratitude,
Jason Liw
